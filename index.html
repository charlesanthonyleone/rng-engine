Every engine you've ever used assumes determinism: same input, same output, forever. RNG Engine flips that. True randomness, with no seeds and no rewind, is the ground everything stands on. Nothing happens the same way twice, and that's the point.

The substrate

No seeds. No rewind.
Every launch is a new universe.

Plant the same kind of tree in the same kind of soil. You do not get the same apple back.

Seeded randomness is a recording: the same seed plays back the same world forever. RNG Engine draws from a non-deterministic substrate instead. Fresh entropy enters at the floor, physics shapes what is possible, and chance chooses what actually happens.

What happened is recorded. What happens next has never happened before.

The engine, layer by layer

Every layer, inverted.

Traditional engines treat randomness as a feature you call. Here it's the floor. Seven layers, built bottom-up, each inheriting chance from the one beneath it.

01Abyssal floor · the ground

Substrate

Memory, math, and the clock itself are non-deterministic. There is no seed to find and no state to replay. This is the core assumption every other engine gets "right," and the one we replace.

ξt = entropy( )No seed in, no replay out.
02The deep · time branches

Clock

Time doesn't tick; it rolls. Every moment branches into possible next moments and one of them becomes real. Time only moves forward.

tn+1 = pick( t′a, t′b, t′c )One branch is taken. None are rewound.
03Midnight water · forks

Simulation

Physics, AI, and anything that ticks forward. Outcomes fork. Throw the same rock twice and you get two histories. Real physics, never replayed.

st+1 ~ K( st, at, ξt )Same throw, sampled within the laws of physics.
04Twilight water · light arrives

Representation

Rendering and audio. A texture loads differently each time, a sound never plays the same. What you see is a moment, not a copy.

rt ~ P( view, st, ξt )Every frame is a fresh sample of the same world.
05Shallows · form takes shape

Structure

The scene graph reorganizes around what's alive. Entities and relations rewire as the world changes. No two instances are identical by default; identity is earned, not assumed.

Gt+1 = reorg( Gt, alivet, ξt )The graph follows life, not a fixed hierarchy.
06The shore · rules meet the tide

Behavior

Scripting and logic. Rules bend under probability. Code describes tendencies, and the world decides.

at ~ π( intent, st, ξt )Intent sets the direction. Chance sets the path.
07The land · where people live

Social

Networking, persistence, player-to-player and player-to-world connections. Shared reality, built from rolls nobody can predict or rewind.

Lt+1 = append( Lt, et )One event, committed once, seen by everyone.
